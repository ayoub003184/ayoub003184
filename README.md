## Featured Projects

### UniAttend

**Technologies:** Python, Face Recognition, GPS, Flask, QR Code

Anti-cheating attendance platform developed for Xiamen University Malaysia.

- Serves more than 1,700 students and 10 lecturers
- Uses GPS-restricted QR codes to verify attendance location
- Supports bulk facial registration and identity verification
- Processes more than 300 students in under two minutes
- Designed to prevent proxy attendance
- Recommended for university-wide deployment

---

### UniBooking

**Technologies:** Node.js, Express, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Railway

Institutional room-booking and timetable-management platform.

- Used by more than 50 lecturers
- Manages 695 weekly timetable entries
- Supports 26 university rooms
- Includes real-time booking-conflict detection
- Validates bookings across a 12-week academic semester
- Maintains complete administrative audit logs

---

### UniMark

**Technologies:** Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Python, OpenCV, PyMuPDF

End-to-end automated multiple-choice examination-marking platform.

- Deployed across the university Foundation Department
- Designed for use by more than 50 lecturers
- Renders examination PDFs at 300 DPI
- Corrects page rotation and deskews scans by up to ±15 degrees
- Detects answer bubbles using Hough Circle processing
- Cross-validates student identification information
- Supports optional PaddleOCR integration
- Exports detailed and summary Excel reports by course learning outcome
- Protected using CSRF controls, bcrypt, rate limiting, and audit logging

---

- Supports real-time visual monitoring and detection
